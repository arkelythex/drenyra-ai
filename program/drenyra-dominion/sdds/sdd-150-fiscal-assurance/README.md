# SDD-150 — Continuous Fiscal Audit and Assurance

> Status: lifecycle:planned · Wave: 5 · Depends on: SDD-090, SDD-100, SDD-140 · Feeds: —

## Capability boundary

Plan independent, read-only assurance over immutable evidence, candidates, approvals, receipts, declarations, and retained decision history. Assurance surfaces findings and traceability to professionals; it never joins approval quorum or mutates the candidate it inspects.

## Required outcome

- Inspection binds to exact immutable identities and preserves tenant isolation.
- Findings distinguish candidate-caused defects from historical or external conditions.
- Supersession retains prior evidence and decisions rather than rewriting history.

## Non-claims

Catalog presence does not implement continuous monitoring, autonomous audit judgment, approval authority, fiscal evidence generation, or external mutation.
