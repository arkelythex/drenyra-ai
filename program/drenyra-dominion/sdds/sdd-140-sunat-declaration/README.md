# SDD-140 — SUNAT Declaration and CDR Reconciliation

> Status: lifecycle:planned · Wave: 5 · Depends on: SDD-050, SDD-070, SDD-110, SDD-130 · Feeds: SDD-150

## Capability boundary

Plan declaration and submission candidates over frozen fiscal evidence, explicit professional approval, restricted adapters, and deterministic policy. Lost or uncertain external responses enter `UNKNOWN` and reconcile against CDR or equivalent acceptance evidence before any retry or completion claim.

## Required outcome

- Human professionals retain filing, fiscal, business, and legal decision authority.
- Submission identity, scope, policy version, approval, response, and reconciliation evidence are receipted.
- Blind retry, inferred acceptance, and memory-shaped evidence fail closed.

## Non-claims

Catalog presence does not provide live SUNAT transport, credentials, filing authority, CDR acceptance, or settled ledger governance.
